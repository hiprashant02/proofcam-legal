Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Your camera work belongs to you. This policy explains the data ProofCam uses, where it goes, and the choices you control.

At a glance
Photos, videos and projects are managed on your device. Location is used for foreground camera and location features, not background tracking. Usage statistics and crash diagnostics default off. Advertising choices are separate.

1. Who we are

ProofCam (Android package com.appvexis.proofcam) is provided by AppVexis Labs. For privacy questions or requests, email support@appvexislabs.com. ProofCam does not require a ProofCam account.

2. Camera, media and project data

With your permission, ProofCam uses the camera and, for videos with audio, microphone. Photos and videos you capture or explicitly import can contain faces, property information, embedded metadata and other personal information. The app processes these locally to apply stamps and stores media, thumbnails, project names, notes, reports and capture metadata on your device. We do not operate a cloud photo library or automatically upload your media to our servers.

Stamps and EXIF metadata may include precise coordinates, address, time, compass direction and available environmental readings. Shared media and reports may reveal these details. Review the export privacy options and the finished file before sharing. Your selected receiving app or recipient handles shared files under its own practices.

3. Location, maps and weather

Location permission is optional. Precise or approximate location is used while the relevant foreground feature is active, including stamping and map features. ProofCam stops continuous camera location updates when the camera is inactive; it does not request background location permission.

  • Maps: Google Maps SDK obtains map content and processes network/device information and map requests under Google's policies. Map snapshots are rendered through the SDK.
  • Addresses: Android's Geocoder sends coordinates to the geocoding service available on your device to resolve an address.
  • Weather: When used, coordinates rounded to four decimal places are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve local weather. As with network services, the provider receives your IP address.

Environmental sensor readings are used locally for overlays when supported. Denying permissions may limit the corresponding feature; you can change permissions in Android Settings.

4. Ads and privacy choices

The free app uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads. The advertising SDK may process identifiers (including the advertising ID where available), IP address, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Ad processing follows your applicable consent choices and Google's policies. ProofCam does not deliberately pass your photos, project notes or stamped GPS coordinates to the ad SDK.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent messages before ads can be requested. Where required, revisit Settings → Privacy & diagnostics → Ad privacy choices. Advertising choices do not enable usage analytics or crash reporting. Verified Pro access removes app ads. Rewarded template access is optional, lasts 24 hours after a confirmed reward and does not itself remove ads.

5. Optional analytics and crash diagnostics

Share usage statistics and Send crash diagnostics default off and can be changed independently in Settings → Privacy & diagnostics. With usage statistics enabled, Firebase Analytics collects app interactions such as successful stamps, template categories and purchase funnel events, along with SDK-generated device/app information and identifiers. Our custom events exclude photos, exact GPS, addresses, project notes and purchase tokens; analytics advertising consent remains denied.

With crash diagnostics enabled, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash stack traces, app/device/OS details and installation identifiers to investigate reliability. Our handled-error reports use operation and error categories rather than original messages or media paths. Disabling diagnostics stops future collection and requests deletion of unsent reports; it does not automatically erase reports already received by Firebase.

6. Purchases, reviews and updates

Google Play handles checkout, payment details, reviews and app updates. We do not receive your payment card details. ProofCam checks purchase ownership and acknowledges completed purchases directly through Google Play on your device. We do not operate a purchase-verification server or receive purchase tokens through our own service. The app caches product identifiers, hashed purchase-token markers and limited-duration access records locally to support purchases and restores. Pending payments do not unlock Pro. Ownership is refreshed when the app runs or a scheduled billing retry executes.

The Play review flow is optional and Google may choose not to display it. The app cannot determine whether you submitted a review. App updates are offered through Google Play; downloaded updates are installed only through the supported update flow.

7. Support

The in-app support form prepares an email in your chosen mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it there. The message includes the topic and text you enter; you can optionally include the app version, Android version and device model. Photos, precise location, logs, identifiers and purchase tokens are not attached automatically. Your email provider and our support mailbox process your address and correspondence to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

8. Retention, deletion and security

Local project data remains until you delete it or clear the app's storage. Uninstalling removes private app storage, but public photos, downloads, manually exported backups and copies shared elsewhere may remain and need separate deletion. Automatic app-data backup/device transfer is excluded by the app's backup rules; manual project backup is under your control.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve your request and for legitimate recordkeeping. Service providers retain their data according to their own settings and policies. We do not promise that uninstalling deletes third-party records. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we control; some records may need to be retained where law requires.

Network integrations use HTTPS and app-private records use Android's application storage protections. Public/shared exports are accessible to their recipients. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-party services and international processing

Providers may process information outside your country under their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — Maps, AdMob, Google Play and related services.
  • Firebase Privacy and Security — optional analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • Open-Meteo privacy information — weather requests.
  • GitHub Privacy Statement — this public policy website. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses.

10. Children, choices and changes

ProofCam is intended for general professional and personal use and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact support. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may update this policy as the app changes; the effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or other appropriate notices.
